# The day behind, as cards

Started 24 Oct 2026 with Friday 23 Oct as the test day. The day behind is
built by hand (no 3 a.m. routine). It is a set of cards, each answering one
question about the day. This file holds what she has said about each card and
what is still hers to decide. The card deck page that compares every version is in her artifacts
(Day Behind Card Deck).

**The whole page is about meaning, and meaningfulness is a metric she wants.**
Her words, 24 Oct: the whole thing is about meaning; "I think the
meaningfulness metric is super important. I just don't know how to get
there." How to measure it is an open decision (below), not a dropped one.

**Where meaning shows, in her words (24 Oct 2026).** "The things that I feel
strongly about are a way of assessing how meaningful something was. And when
I'm able to think about it and reframe and put things in context, it's a way
of highlighting meaning, a way of noticing it." Two signals, then:
- **strength of feeling** — how strongly she felt about a moment (the row's
  feeling, deduced from her words, J6; the words she reached for);
- **reflection** — a moment she came back to, reframed or put in context (an
  insight or journal row on it, a later row that names it, words that place it
  in a bigger story).
Not yet a formula; the measure is still hers to shape.

**How the page reads.** Her word, 24 Oct, on The Whole Map: everything at a
glance, and scrolling through it the way that page scrolls (a section's label
stays in view down its side while its cards scroll past).

## How the day went: concentric rings (24 Oct, second pass)

Her words on the first pass (seven slices, one minute in one slice): forcing
things into one category is part of the issue; moving and fun overlap and she
doesn't know how much. And the colours: natural pastels, not a colour-blind-safe
set ("this is for me"), building on the palette she started.

So: one ring per thing she tracks, out from the center, overlapping freely, on
a 24-hour clock with midnight at the top. Prototype on Friday: the How Friday Went page in her artifacts.

1. 😴 sleep
2. 🍽️ food: making it, and eating and drinking
3. 🚶‍♀️ moving (movement and time outside)
4. 🌗 dawn and dusk: each flow's steps, on a pale band from its first step to its last (the time set aside for morning and evening practice)
5. 💼 working: work, and be•do (working on the system)
6. 📱 in front of me: phone, laptop, TV, a book ("am I on my phone, am I on the TV, am I reading")
~~7. with people~~ — moved out, third pass (below)

**Third pass, same day.** Her words: a great step forward, it shows a lot and
she loves that; make it even simpler. No emoji around the dial (the moments on
the rim and the emoji ring labels are gone; each ring carries a short word
label at the top). Food is one colour. **With people leaves this card** and
becomes its own card, a sociogram. Kitchen cleanup is not counted as food; she
thought that fair. She likes seeing dawn and dusk, and seeing when she worked.

Pastels: misty blue sleep, apricot and clay food, sage moving, butter dawn and
lavender dusk (her flow colours, softened), eucalyptus work and olive be•do,
dusty rose phone, slate laptop, orchid TV, wheat book, terracotta people.

**Fourth pass, same day.** Tighter. Sleep is the core, a filled disc in the
middle ("the most important thing"). One colour per ring, never several ("too
confusing to my eye"); where a ring holds two things, a pattern tells them
apart (be•do work is the striped part of working). Dawn and dusk share one
colour; the clock says which is which. Screens are one thing: on a screen or
not (phone, laptop, TV; a paper book is not a screen).

**Fifth pass, same day.** The order from the core out: sleep (the core),
flows (dawn and dusk practice, one ring, no need to name which), food,
moving, working, other ("there's a lot of things I'm doing that aren't those
things"). Other is time a row covers that none of the rings inside it claims.
Screens are the outermost circle, a thin line: on a screen or not, black in
light mode, white in dark.

**Sixth pass, same day.** Food is food only: meals, snacks and making them, no
drinks. Flows never overlap sleep: a flow step logged while asleep (a backup
close at 11:59 PM) is left off, and each flow's span runs only through awake
time. Trying her idea: sleep and the flows share the core, since they never
share a minute (sleep solid blue; each flow a pale wedge from its first step
to its last, its steps in full colour).

**Seventh pass, same day.** A small hollow at the very center, left open for
now ("the center should be something but I don't know what"). Moving and
eating: she should be sitting to eat, so a meal's minutes come out of the
moving ring (Friday's lunch on a bench mid-walk is a gap in moving); making
food is food, not movement.

**Eighth pass, same day.** Food and moving share one ring, called body for
now (they no longer overlap, since a meal comes out of moving). Working shows
her kinds of work: professional work, be•do (striped), and caretaking ("caretaking
is work"): the care of the child, the house and the dogs — school runs, pickup,
bedtime, kitchen cleanup. On the working ring professional work wins a shared
minute, then caretaking, then be•do: a be•do session running in the background
during a school run is still the school run. Time together that isn't care work
(reading together) stays in other.

**Ninth pass, same day.** Her words: "the best one yet". Rules:
- **Her doing, not the system's.** "If the laptop is running that's not me
  working. It's what I'm actually doing." A session row records when the
  laptop ran; its minutes count as her work only while she wasn't driving,
  eating, moving or asleep. (25 Oct 2026: a session row now names her
  stretches, from her own messages, and is drawn over those only; this rule
  still guards a row that names none.)
- **One shade per ring, never several colours on one line.** Each ring has its
  own hue and a very light tint of it as its track; its parts differ by
  pattern: core (blue) sleep solid, flows dotted; body (sage) food solid,
  moving striped; working (clay) professional solid, caretaking striped,
  be•do dotted; other (sand) solid. Four shades plus the screen line.
- **No labels on the dial.** Later: small icons, like the ones made for the
  wellness wheel.

**Tenth pass, where 24 Oct ended.** "About as good as we're going to get it
today. It looks really great." Thicker rings, easier to tap. Patterns finer and
more detailed, each chunk outlined in a darker shade of its ring. The flows'

**What the clock is for, in her words (24 Oct 2026, thinking out loud).** "What
I'm trying to measure or show is maybe efficiency: how well am I spending my
time?" Her targets, which will change, perhaps with Oura: sleeping well for 8
hours; moving well for 2 hours; fixing one really good meal a day, and sitting
down to eat with intention; getting work done (effective at work) and effective
at home. "When I look at this clock I'm thinking: was I efficient. And that
does matter."

Proposed, not yet hers: each ring carries its target as a mark on the ring (8 h
of sleep, 2 h of moving), set each morning and perhaps eased by readiness; the
hollow center answers the question, how well the day was spent against those
targets. Meals would need two things the rows don't yet say every time: whether
the meal was made, and whether it was eaten sitting down with intention (backlog
261003_1433 already asks for meals logged with how they were made and whether
she ate with intention).

**The waking day, 24 Oct (simpler).** "I think I just made it too complicated.
Ideally I'm in bed for 8 hours", and could the dial just show the 16 waking

**The rainbow, 24 Oct.** "This is really working for me." Shades, not

**Being and doing, 24 Oct.** She kept getting confused about the split ("what

down with intention is being; reading together is being, even when it is also
care; shaping be•do in a work session is doing. She wants the shape kept and
being and doing as their own concentric bands:
- inside: the dawn and dusk flows (sunrise yellow, sunset violet)
- **being**, cool sage-teal shades: eating and drinking (deepest), moving, recreation (lightest)
- **doing**, warm clay shades: professional work (deepest), caretaking (others and herself), cooking, be•do (lightest)
