# The day behind, as cards

Started 24 Oct 2026 with Friday 23 Oct as the test day. The day behind is
built by hand (no 3 a.m. routine). It is a set of cards, each answering one
question about the day. This file holds what she has said about each card and
what is still hers to decide. The card deck page that compares every version is in her artifacts
(Day Behind Card Deck).

**The whole page is about meaning, and meaningfulness is a metric she wants.**
Her words, 24 Oct: the whole thing is about meaning; "I think the
meaningfulness metric is super important. I just don't know how to get
there." How to measure it is an open decision (below), not a dropped one.

**Where meaning shows, in her words (24 Oct 2026).** "The things that I feel
strongly about are a way of assessing how meaningful something was. And when
I'm able to think about it and reframe and put things in context, it's a way
of highlighting meaning, a way of noticing it." Two signals, then:
- **strength of feeling** — how strongly she felt about a moment (the row's
  feeling, deduced from her words, J6; the words she reached for);
- **reflection** — a moment she came back to, reframed or put in context (an
  insight or journal row on it, a later row that names it, words that place it
  in a bigger story).
Not yet a formula; the measure is still hers to shape.

**How the page reads.** Her word, 24 Oct, on The Whole Map: everything at a
glance, and scrolling through it the way that page scrolls (a section's label
stays in view down its side while its cards scroll past).

## How the day went: concentric rings (24 Oct, second pass)

Her words on the first pass (seven slices, one minute in one slice): forcing
things into one category is part of the issue; moving and fun overlap and she
doesn't know how much. And the colours: natural pastels, not a colour-blind-safe
set ("this is for me"), building on the palette she started.

So: one ring per thing she tracks, out from the center, overlapping freely, on
a 24-hour clock with midnight at the top. Prototype on Friday: the How Friday Went page in her artifacts.

1. 😴 sleep
2. 🍽️ food: making it, and eating and drinking
3. 🚶‍♀️ moving (movement and time outside)
4. 🌗 dawn and dusk: each flow's steps, on a pale band from its first step to its last (the time set aside for morning and evening practice)
5. 💼 working: work, and be•do (working on the system)
6. 📱 in front of me: phone, laptop, TV, a book ("am I on my phone, am I on the TV, am I reading")
~~7. with people~~ — moved out, third pass (below)

**Third pass, same day.** Her words: a great step forward, it shows a lot and
she loves that; make it even simpler. No emoji around the dial (the moments on
the rim and the emoji ring labels are gone; each ring carries a short word
label at the top). Food is one colour. **With people leaves this card** and
becomes its own card, a sociogram. Kitchen cleanup is not counted as food; she
thought that fair. She likes seeing dawn and dusk, and seeing when she worked.

Pastels: misty blue sleep, apricot and clay food, sage moving, butter dawn and
lavender dusk (her flow colours, softened), eucalyptus work and olive be•do,
dusty rose phone, slate laptop, orchid TV, wheat book, terracotta people.

**Fourth pass, same day.** Tighter. Sleep is the core, a filled disc in the
middle ("the most important thing"). One colour per ring, never several ("too
confusing to my eye"); where a ring holds two things, a pattern tells them
apart (be•do work is the striped part of working). Dawn and dusk share one
colour; the clock says which is which. Screens are one thing: on a screen or
not (phone, laptop, TV; a paper book is not a screen).

**Fifth pass, same day.** The order from the core out: sleep (the core),
flows (dawn and dusk practice, one ring, no need to name which), food,
moving, working, other ("there's a lot of things I'm doing that aren't those
things"). Other is time a row covers that none of the rings inside it claims.
Screens are the outermost circle, a thin line: on a screen or not, black in
light mode, white in dark.
