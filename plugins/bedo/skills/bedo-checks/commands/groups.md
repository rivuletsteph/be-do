# `groups`

**When:** every 👁️ look ahead, with no one asking: the my day page's builder runs it on the rows it already read and draws *could go together* at the bottom of the page (26 Nov 2026). With `--mail`, at the 📧 clear gmail inbox, on the threads that step read. And on call.

**Command:** `groups [--rows rows.json] [--mail threads.json] [--json]`

**Her open actions, read side by side** (24 Nov 2026, her words: I need you to keep track of these open intentions and potential actions and proactively scan how these things can be grouped). That day the live base held some 440 open rows and nothing read them together.

Every open ⚡ row (▫️ potential, ⬜ intention, ▶️ in motion), this week's base and last week's, one per key, the live base first. Duet's asks for Code (`for code:`) and her hidden drives are left out. Four lenses, a group needs two rows:

- **one sitting**: the same kind of doing whatever the drive: 📞 calls and texts, ✉️ email and posts, 🗓️ bookings and sign-ups, 🧾 forms and bills, 🛒 orders and errands, 🔎 look-ups. From the title's own words.
- **one person**: the same person on the person or mentioned field. The household (her, and the people in her settings) is with her by default and never a group; nor is anyone on a tenth of everything.
- **one place**: a town or spot the title names (`in Riverton`, `at Oak Hall`), and any word in her `groups.places` setting.
- **one drive**: two or more ⬜ steps waiting on the same drive.

Each group lists what she meant first (⬜), then what is started, then the maybes. Four groups a lens at most (all six kinds of sitting).

**Offers, never a plan.** Nothing is written, moved, dated or closed. She picks; a pick goes on the day the usual way (`--pick`, `--session`).

**The mail half** (her words, same day: it did not make it into my system, the ask came in by work email). With `--mail threads.json` (the Gmail connector's `search_threads` result, as the 📧 step read it) it names each thread that reads as an ask of her (a question, *can you*, *please*, *let me know*), from a person and not a list, that no row in the two bases mentions, by its subject's words or its sender's name. A live read then reads every row, closed ones too, so an ask already done is not flagged.

- **Work mail is seen only where it reaches Gmail**: forwarded from her work address, or a chat or calendar notice. Her work mailbox itself is not read.
- Her own addresses (`groups.my_mail`) are never a sender to flag; a thread to or from `groups.work_mail` is marked *work*.
- **A flagged ask goes on her short list**, in plain words with its link: *this came by email and isn't on your list — add it?* A row is written only on her yes (J3: an intention is hers).

Settings, all optional, in the `facts_local` row: `"groups": {"places": [...], "my_mail": [...], "work_mail": [...]}`. The page reads `household` from `day_ahead_local` too, beside `self_person` and `calendar_people`.
